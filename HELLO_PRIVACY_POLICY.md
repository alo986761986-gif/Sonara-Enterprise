## 1. Information HELLO may process

Depending on the features you choose to use, HELLO may process:

- **Account and profile information**, such as display name, username, email address, optional phone number, profile photo, status/bio, and authentication identifiers.
- **User-generated content**, such as messages, media, files, and other content you choose to send or store through HELLO.
- **Camera and microphone data** only when you choose features that require camera, audio, calling, recording, or media capture access and grant the relevant Android permission.
- **Location data** only when you choose a location or live-location feature and grant the relevant Android location permission.
- **Technical and security information** necessary to operate the app, authenticate users, maintain sessions, deliver notifications, protect the service, and diagnose operational problems.

HELLO does not use an Advertising ID for advertising and does not display third-party advertising.

## 2. How information is used

Information is processed as necessary to:

- create, authenticate, secure, and manage HELLO accounts;
- provide profiles, messaging, media, calling, location-sharing, and related communication features selected by the user;
- synchronize app data and deliver notifications;
- prevent abuse, fraud, unauthorized access, and security incidents;
- maintain, troubleshoot, and improve reliability of the service;
- respond to support, privacy, and account-deletion requests.

## 3. Service providers

HELLO uses Google/Firebase services to provide core application functionality, including services such as Firebase Authentication and Firebase data/storage and application-security services. Google services may process information according to their own applicable privacy terms and policies when providing these services.

HELLO may also use Android and Google platform services required for authentication, device integrity, location features, notifications, QR/code scanning, translation, and other user-selected app functionality.

## 4. Sharing of information

HELLO does not sell personal information and does not share personal information with advertisers for targeted advertising.

Information may be processed by service providers only where needed to provide, secure, maintain, or support HELLO, or where disclosure is required by applicable law or necessary to protect users and the service.

## 5. Data retention

Account and service data is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide HELLO, maintain security and integrity, satisfy legitimate support needs, or meet applicable legal obligations.

When an account-deletion request is completed, the account and associated personal data are deleted unless specific information must be retained for a legitimate security, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution, or legal reason. Any such retention is limited to what is necessary for that purpose.

## 6. Permissions and user choice

Android permissions such as camera, microphone, notifications, and location are requested only when required by relevant functionality. Users can manage granted permissions in Android device settings. Disabling a permission may prevent the related HELLO feature from working.

## 7. Security

HELLO uses reasonable technical and organizational measures, together with platform and Firebase security capabilities, to protect account and application data. No electronic system can guarantee absolute security.

## 8. Children

HELLO is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information improperly, contact us so the matter can be reviewed and appropriate action taken.

## 9. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when HELLO features, data practices, legal requirements, or service providers change. The date at the top of this page identifies the latest revision.
