## 1. Zero-tolerance policy

HELLO has a zero-tolerance policy for **child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE)** and for **child sexual abuse material (CSAM)**.

Users must not use HELLO to create, upload, send, receive, store, request, promote, distribute, facilitate, or otherwise engage with content or conduct that sexually exploits or abuses minors.

Prohibited conduct includes, without limitation:

- sexual content involving minors;
- grooming, solicitation, coercion, sextortion, or sexual exploitation of minors;
- requests for sexual images or videos of minors;
- sharing, trading, selling, or distributing CSAM;
- encouraging or facilitating sexual abuse or exploitation of children;
- using HELLO to arrange, coordinate, or promote conduct that puts a child at risk of sexual harm.

## 2. Reporting concerns

## 3. Enforcement

HELLO may take action against accounts, content, or activity that violates these standards. Depending on the circumstances, actions may include:

- restricting or removing content;
- limiting account functionality;
- suspending or terminating accounts;
- preserving relevant information where legally required;
- cooperating with competent authorities or legally authorized child-safety organizations when required by applicable law.

## 4. Handling suspected CSAM

When HELLO becomes aware of apparent CSAM or credible child sexual exploitation activity, the matter is reviewed and handled in accordance with applicable law and platform obligations.

HELLO does not knowingly permit CSAM to remain available through the service. Where legally required, apparent CSAM or related activity may be reported to the appropriate authorities or legally designated reporting organizations.

## 5. Compliance with child-safety laws

HELLO is committed to complying with applicable child-safety, online-safety, privacy, and reporting laws in the jurisdictions where the service is offered.

We may update our processes, product safeguards, and these standards as legal and platform requirements evolve.

## 6. User safety expectations

Users must not:

- impersonate a minor for sexual purposes;
- contact minors for sexual exploitation or grooming;
- pressure or threaten minors to provide sexual content;
- distribute intimate imagery of minors;
- attempt to evade child-safety enforcement measures.

Users should immediately stop engaging with suspicious content or accounts and report the concern through HELLO's safety channels.
